Your 2026 Zanzibar Arrival Guide.
Land smooth, pay fair, skip the stress.

Insurance, Terminal 3, cash & cards, SIM/eSIM, and honest transfer prices — everything we wish every arriving traveller knew before touchdown.

1. Mandatory travel insurance (no opt-out)

Zanzibar requires inbound travel insurance — effective 1 October 2024, administered by the Zanzibar Insurance Corporation (ZIC).

  • Adults: USD 44 per person · Children 3–17: USD 22 · Coverage ~92 days
  • Arrive with the QR code ready — you'll be asked at immigration
  • We remind you of it before you fly (and never resell it — use the official ZIC channel only)

2. Landing at Abeid Amani Karume Airport (Terminal 3)

  1. Immigration — passport + ZIC insurance QR. Keep it on your phone.
  2. Baggage → Customs — smooth, straight forward out.
  3. The exit zone — this is where street taxis pounce. Smile, walk past the crowd, meet your pre-arranged driver (or grab the airport shuttle).
  4. SIM / cash — Airtel/Vodacom/Halotel top-up kiosks and currency exchange are available; we can build a free SIM/ATM stop into your transfer.

4. Cash, cards & mobile money

  • Zanzibar is a largely cash economy — carry USD and local TZS, especially for small vendors.
  • Island ATMs sometimes run dry or reject foreign Visa/Mastercard — keep a card with a low foreign fee as backup and withdraw early.
  • For any service, agree the price in writing before you commit (a WhatsApp message counts).

5. SIM / eSIM & connectivity

Airtel, Vodacom and Halotel sell tourist SIMs at the airport and in Stone Town. eSIM from your phone's provider also works on the island — WhatsApp runs everywhere, and it's how you'll reach your driver, your hotel, and us.
