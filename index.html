Why travellers choose a pre-arranged transfer

Zanzibar arrivals consistently report airport-taxi overcharging, unmetered fares and erratic quotes ($30–$80 for the same ride) as the #1 friction of the trip. A fixed, pre-agreed private transfer is the lowest-risk, highest-trust way to land — and it frees you to enjoy what you came for.

Frequently asked questions

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. You see the total before you travel, in the quote confirmation. No hidden airport fees, no haggling, no on-the-spot surcharges.

Can you wait if my flight is delayed?

Yes — we track your flight and the wait is free. Overruns and delays don't cost extra.

Who drives me?

A vetted, licensed driver. You receive their name, photo, and vehicle registration 24 hours before arrival.

Do I need Zanzibar's mandatory insurance?

Yes — Zanzibar has required inbound travel insurance (ZIC) since 1 Oct 2024. We remind you of the QR before you fly. See the Arrival Guide.

Can you take me to Nungwi, Paje, Stone Town or Kendwa?

All covered — see route prices above. One private vehicle, four adults, luggage included.
